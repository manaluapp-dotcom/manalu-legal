English

Space Regatta is a free turn-based space racing game for players aged 13 and older. It needs no account. The game is funded by advertising.

  • What the game itself stores. The current race, its backup, your settings and your progress (wins, tour stars, journal, unlocked ships and colours) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. There are no accounts, no chat and no social features. We, Manalu Apps, receive no personal data about you.
  • Advertising. The game shows adverts provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a full-screen advert that may appear between races, and a video you choose to watch to unlock a ship, a trail colour, a tour stage or a second counted attempt at the race of the day. To deliver and measure adverts and to prevent fraud, Google automatically collects and processes: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID and the app set ID), interactions with the app and with adverts (app launches, taps, video views) and diagnostic information. This data goes directly to Google and is encrypted in transit; it is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Your choices about adverts. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, and you can change that choice at any time: Pause → Rules → About → "Advert privacy settings". On any device you can reset or delete the advertising ID, or turn off advert personalisation, in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Without consent you may still see non-personalised adverts.
  • No purchases. The game sells nothing and asks for no payment details.
  • Network. The app connects to the internet only to load adverts and the consent form. The game itself — races against bots, the tour, race codes and the race of the day — works offline. A shared race code is copied to your clipboard by you and contains nothing but the track number.
  • Children. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
  • Your rights. We keep no data about you outside your device, so uninstalling the app removes everything the game stored. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google's own tools. Questions: manalu.app@gmail.com.
  • Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new update date.
