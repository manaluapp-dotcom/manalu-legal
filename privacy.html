English

Sudimals is a children's sudoku game with animals. We built it so that it collects no data at all.

  • No personal data is collected. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information — no names, no e-mail addresses, no photos, no location, no contacts.
  • No account is needed. There is no registration or sign-in.
  • No internet activity. The game works fully offline. It makes no network requests, contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • The only exception is the store purchase. If a parent chooses to buy the full version, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. The app itself never sees or stores any payment information.
  • Game progress stays on the device. Completed levels and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Purchases are protected by a parental gate. A child cannot reach the purchase screen without an adult solving an arithmetic task first.

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or delete. Questions about this policy: manalu.app@gmail.com
