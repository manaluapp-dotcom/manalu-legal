English

Takewell is a medication reminder and tracking app. By default your health data stays on your device.

  • Your health data stays on your device. Medications, schedules, intake marks, notes, doctor appointments and family profiles are stored locally in the app's own database and are not sent anywhere by default.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose to sign in to enable cloud backup, we receive your e-mail address and account identifier (Firebase Authentication), and a copy of your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, accessible only to your account. Without signing in, the app works fully offline.
  • Subscription data. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. To verify subscription status we use RevenueCat, which receives a technical purchase identifier and an anonymous app identifier (or your account identifier if you are signed in). Payment details are handled solely by Google Play — the app never sees or stores them.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app shows no advertising and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We never sell or share your data.
  • Reminders are local. Notifications are scheduled on your device and do not pass through any server.
  • Deleting your data. Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. To delete a cloud backup, sign out and write to us, or revoke access in your Google account settings.
  • Third parties: Google Play Billing (payments), Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (sign-in and backup, only if you sign in), RevenueCat (subscription status).
  • Not a medical device. Takewell only provides reminders and record-keeping; it gives no medical advice. Your treatment is prescribed by your doctor.
