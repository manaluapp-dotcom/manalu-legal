English

Frog Bop is a casual tap-the-frog reaction game for children and families. It takes part in the Google Play Families program.

  • The game collects no personal data. Best scores and settings (sound, language) are stored only on your device and never leave it. There are no accounts, no chat, no social features and no external links inside the game.
  • Child-safe advertising only. Ads are served by Google AdMob with child-directed treatment: only non-personalized, contextual ads with a G content rating. The game removes the Android advertising-ID permission (AD_ID), so the advertising ID is never shared. No behavioral profiling, no remarketing.
  • Technical data processed by Google. To deliver and secure ads, Google may process technical information such as IP address (which can imply approximate location), device type and diagnostics — see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • "Remove Ads" purchase. The one-time purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under the purchaser's Google account. We never see or store payment details. Parents can enable purchase authentication in Google Play settings.
  • Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Questions about this policy: manalu.app@gmail.com
