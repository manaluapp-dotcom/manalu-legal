English

Bebimo is a keepsake app for parents about their baby's first year: a photo album by month, a milestone checklist, growth charts, a teeth chart, a vaccination calendar, a feeding guide, white noise and monthiversary reminders. It is made for adults (parents and caregivers), needs no account and works fully offline.

  • We collect no data. Everything you enter — the child's name, sex, date and time of birth, place of birth, birth weight and length, gestation weeks, measurements, milestones, notes, teeth, vaccinations, photos and your settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers and no access to it.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads. There is no registration or sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. The release build declares no Internet permission.
  • Photos. Photos are chosen through the system photo picker or camera app; the app itself asks for no access to your photo library. Chosen photos are compressed and kept in the app's private storage. They leave your device only when you explicitly export them: by sharing a card or collage, creating a PDF year book, or making a ZIP backup. Where you send those files is up to you.
  • The only exception is the store purchase. If you buy the full version, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. The app never sees or stores any payment information; it only learns whether the purchase was made.
  • Reminders. Monthiversary reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are optional and use no push service. You may be asked for the notification permission; the app works without it.
  • Backups. The ZIP backup you can create in the app is a plain file saved where you choose. It contains the child's data and photos, so treat it as you would any private file.
  • Children. The app is intended for adults and stores information about a child only because the parent enters it, only on the parent's own device. We do not knowingly collect data from children and receive no data about them.
  • Health information. Growth charts, milestones and the feeding guide are general reference material based on public sources (WHO, CDC, the Ministry of Health of Ukraine). They are not medical advice and do not replace a pediatrician. Values you enter stay on your device.
  • Deletion. Delete a child's profile inside the app to remove all its data and photos, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Backups you exported remain under your control.
  • Your rights. Because we keep no data about you outside your device, access or deletion requests are unnecessary. Questions: manalu.app@gmail.com.
  • Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new update date.
